# Disclaimer

LedgerTrace reconstructs historical ledger states, identifies the
transaction sequence that caused a financial discrepancy, and flags
integrity failures for professional review. It does not post to the
general ledger and does not certify GAAP, IFRS, or SOX compliance.

All outputs are unposted workpapers for review only, not a compliance
certificate. A qualified professional must evaluate source completeness and
each proposed adjustment. The authors are not the user's CPA.

Forbidden marketing claims: SOX-ready, GAAP-certified, audit-complete,
posts to QBO. Do not describe the product using those claims.
