# V1 scope freeze

## In V1
Local CSV ingest, replay, D1-D5 and unbalanced_entry, proposed matches,
evidence JSON/PDF, local API, three-screen UI: Upload, Findings, Evidence.

## Explicitly out until a paying user requests it in writing
QuickBooks / Xero / NetSuite / Plaid connectors; posting or authoritative-looking
journal exports; US GAAP / IFRS rule packs (ASC 606, IFRS 15, leases, inventory);
multi-currency translation; Electron packaging; multi-tenant cloud;
LLMs inside detectors; auto-fix that writes the GL.

## First allowed V2 after revenue
One narrow check a customer requested in writing. Still no posting.

## Authorized V2.1 exception
Optional statement-ending claim test, generic/QBO/Xero CSV header dialects, and
conditional unposted draft journals in evidence JSON/PDF. This is not a connector,
posting endpoint, GL journal export, or accounting standards pack. V2.2 is out.
